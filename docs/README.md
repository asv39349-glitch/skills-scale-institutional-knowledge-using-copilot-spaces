# OctoAcme Project Management Processes

OctoAcme uses a collaborative, iterative approach to deliver customer and business value. Projects begin by validating the need, defining measurable outcomes, identifying stakeholders, and aligning on whether to proceed. The team then turns approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, milestones, estimates, dependencies, and a Definition of Done.

During execution, the team tracks work on a project board, communicates progress and blockers regularly, manages risks, and validates quality through tests, reviews, and QA. Releases are planned with passing CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, and rollback or mitigation plans. After sprints, releases, or incidents, retrospectives capture lessons and turn them into owned improvement actions. Clear roles, stakeholder communication, and ongoing measurement support each stage.
